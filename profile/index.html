About

From the shop floor to the bank's front line

For over 25 years across the GCC, my work has centred on one thing: people. I started in retail, rising from sales representative to Department Store Manager at Lulu Hypermarket in Qatar, where I led 80 team members and ran categories turning over 500K AED a day. I later built an e-commerce division from scratch as Operations Manager at 88 Star Trading.

In 2024 I brought that customer-first mindset into banking. At Commercial Bank of Dubai I retained credit card customers using balance transfers, easy payment plans, APR negotiation and fee waivers. Today, as Retention Officer at Dubai Islamic Bank, I protect salary-transfer and deposit relationships with Sharia-compliant Wakala and E-Savings solutions, FX remittances, alt digital banking and Walaa Rewards.

What sets me apart is the mix of floor-level sales instinct and a data-driven approach. I build my own retention trackers, disposition frameworks, MIS dashboards and product guides, and I use AI tools to work faster and stay current.

“Every account that stays is a relationship someone chose to keep. My job is to give them a reason.”Approach: understand the trigger, match the product to the life, follow up until it is resolved.
